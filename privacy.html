最後更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

重點
  • 我們只收集安排的士行程、保障安全及處理投訴所需的資料。

  • 我們不會出售你的資料，不會用作廣告，App 內亦沒有廣告或第三方追蹤工具。
  • 你可隨時要求查閱、更正或刪除你的資料。

1. 我們是誰

TaxiBot HK（「我們」）是香港的叫車平台，為乘客聯絡持牌的士司機。本政策適用於「TaxiBot 叫車」及「TaxiBot 司機」App、我們的 Telegram 及 WhatsApp 叫車機械人，以及 taxibothk.com 網站（統稱「服務」）。我們不是的士營辦商，行程由司機提供。

我們按香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）處理個人資料。

2. 我們收集的資料

所有用戶

  • 帳戶資料：名稱、語言設定；如以電話登入，你的手提電話號碼；如經 Telegram 或 WhatsApp 使用，該平台提供的用戶 ID 及名稱（WhatsApp 包括你的電話號碼）。
  • 對話及相片：你與機械人、司機或乘客之間傳送的文字、相片及位置。
  • 意見及投訴：你透過「意見反映」或評分提交的內容。
  • 裝置資料：推送通知所需的裝置代碼、App 版本及基本錯誤記錄。

乘客

  • 上車點、目的地、中途站、預約時間、人數、行李、特別需要及給司機的備註。
  • 位置：只在你使用 App 時，並經你允許，用作把你所在位置設為上車點，及在行程中讓司機找到你。我們不會在背景追蹤你的位置。
  • 行程紀錄：車資估算、貼士、行程狀態、評分。

司機

  • 姓名、手提電話、電郵（如提供，用於獎賞）。
  • 駕駛執照號碼及有效期、的士司機證號碼及到期日、的士車牌、車款及的士類別，以及駕駛執照和的士司機證的相片。
  • 位置：只在你上線接單並使用 App 時，用作配對附近訂單及讓乘客在即時地圖看到的士位置。
  • 接單、完成及取消紀錄、乘客評分。

我們不會收集你的香港身份證副本或號碼，亦不會收集信用卡或銀行資料（車資直接付給司機）。

3. 我們如何使用資料

  • 為乘客配對司機、顯示車資估算、到達時間及即時地圖。
  • 讓乘客與司機在行程期間互相聯絡（訊息及 App 內通話）。
  • 核實司機身份及資格。
  • 發送行程通知及登入驗證碼。
  • 保障安全、防止濫用及欺詐、處理投訴及失物。
  • 計算司機獎賞，及改善服務（例如統計訂單數量）。

如要把資料用於上述以外的新用途，我們會先取得你的同意。

4. 誰會看到你的資料

  • 行程的另一方：司機接單後會看到乘客的名稱、上落車點、備註及訊息；乘客會看到司機名稱、車牌、車款、位置及訊息。司機只可為完成該次行程使用乘客資料。
  • 我們的服務供應商，只按我們的指示處理資料：
    • Telegram 及 Meta（WhatsApp）：如你經這些平台使用服務，訊息會經它們傳送。
    • Twilio：發送短訊驗證碼。
    • Cloudflare：網絡傳輸，及在部分網絡下為 App 內通話轉駁。通話不會被錄音。
    • Expo、Apple 及 Google：App 更新及推送通知。
    • 地圖服務（OpenStreetMap、OSRM、高德地圖、Apple 地圖或 Google 地圖）：搜尋地點、計算路線及顯示地圖。發送的是地點座標，不包括你的名稱或電話。
  • 法律要求：如法律要求，或為保障人身安全（例如配合警方調查），我們可能向有關當局提供資料。

我們的伺服器及部分供應商的伺服器位於香港以外地區（例如美國）。我們只會選用有合理保安措施的供應商。

6. 保安

資料儲存在受保護的伺服器上，經加密連線（HTTPS）傳送；只有獲授權的營運人員可查閱，例如審核司機申請或處理投訴。沒有任何系統完全安全；如發生影響你的資料外洩，我們會盡快通知你及有關當局。

7. 你的權利

  • 查閱及更正：你可要求查閱我們持有的你的資料，並更正不準確的地方。
  • 刪除帳戶：你可要求刪除帳戶及相關資料。我們會在 30 日內處理，法律要求保留或正處理中的投訴資料除外。
  • 撤回權限：你可隨時在手機設定關閉位置或通知權限；部分功能（例如以所在位置作上車點）將無法使用。
  • 直接促銷：我們不會用你的資料作直接促銷。

提出要求：在 App 或機械人內按「意見反映」，或電郵至 admin@taxibothk.com。我們可能需要核實你的身份，並會在 40 日內回覆。

8. 兒童

服務並非為 18 歲以下人士而設。未滿 18 歲人士應由家長或監護人代為叫車。

9. 政策更新

如本政策有重大修改，我們會在 App 或機械人內通知你，並更新本頁的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short
  • We collect only what we need to arrange taxi trips, keep people safe and handle complaints.
  • Chats between passenger and driver are deleted automatically about 1 hour after the trip.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. The apps have no ads and no third-party trackers.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

TaxiBot HK ("we") is a Hong Kong taxi booking service that connects passengers with licensed taxi drivers. This policy covers the TaxiBot 叫車 (passenger) and TaxiBot 司機 (driver) apps, our Telegram and WhatsApp booking bots, and the taxibothk.com website (together, the "Service"). We are not a taxi operator: the trip is provided by the driver.

We handle personal data under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486).

2. What we collect

Everyone

  • Account details: name and language; your mobile number if you sign in by phone; if you use Telegram or WhatsApp, the user ID and name that platform gives us (WhatsApp includes your phone number).
  • Messages and photos: text, photos and locations you send to the bot, your driver or your passenger.
  • Feedback and complaints: what you send through Feedback or ratings.
  • Device details: a push notification token, the app version and basic error logs.

Passengers

  • Pickup, destination, stops, booking time, number of passengers, luggage, special needs and notes to the driver.
  • Location: only while you use the app and with your permission, to set your pickup to where you are and to help the driver find you during the trip. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Trip records: fare estimate, tip, trip status and ratings.

Drivers

  • Name, mobile number and email (if given, for rewards).
  • Driving licence number and expiry date, Taxi Driver Identity Plate number and expiry date, taxi plate, car model and taxi type, and photos of your driving licence and Taxi Driver Identity Plate.
  • Location: only while you're online for orders and using the app, to match nearby orders and show passengers where your taxi is.
  • Records of orders taken, completed and cancelled, and passenger ratings.

We do not collect copies or numbers of your Hong Kong Identity Card, or card or bank details (fares are paid to the driver directly).

3. How we use it

  • To match passengers with drivers and show fare estimates, arrival times and the live map.
  • To let passenger and driver contact each other during the trip (messages and in-app calls).
  • To check drivers' identity and eligibility.
  • To send trip notifications and sign-in codes.
  • For safety, to prevent abuse and fraud, and to handle complaints and lost property.
  • To calculate driver rewards and improve the Service (for example, counting orders).

We will ask for your consent before using your data for any new purpose.

4. Who sees it

  • The other side of your trip: once a driver takes an order, they see the passenger's name, pickup and drop-off, notes and messages; the passenger sees the driver's name, plate, car, location and messages. Drivers may use passenger details only to complete that trip.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our instructions:
    • Telegram and Meta (WhatsApp): if you use the Service there, messages pass through them.
    • Twilio: sending SMS sign-in codes.
    • Cloudflare: network delivery, and relaying in-app calls on some networks. Calls are not recorded.
    • Expo, Apple and Google: app updates and push notifications.
    • Map services (OpenStreetMap, OSRM, AMap, Apple Maps or Google Maps): place search, routes and maps. They receive coordinates, not your name or phone number.
  • When the law requires it: we may give data to the authorities when required by law or to protect someone's safety (for example, a police investigation).

Our server and some providers' servers are outside Hong Kong (for example, in the United States). We use only providers with reasonable security measures.

6. Security

Data is stored on protected servers and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only authorised staff can see it, for example to review driver applications or handle complaints. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as soon as we can.

7. Your rights

  • Access and correction: you can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you and have mistakes corrected.
  • Delete your account: you can ask us to delete your account and its data. We do this within 30 days, except data the law requires us to keep or that is part of an open complaint.
  • Permissions: you can turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings at any time; some features (such as using your location as the pickup) then won't work.
  • Direct marketing: we don't use your data for direct marketing.

To make a request, use Feedback in the app or bot, or email admin@taxibothk.com. We may need to confirm who you are, and we reply within 40 days.

8. Children

The Service is not meant for people under 18. They should have a parent or guardian book for them.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we'll tell you in the app or bot and update the date on this page.
