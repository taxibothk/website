Connect the WhatsApp Business app number

For TaxiBot staff only. Have the phone with the WhatsApp Business app (latest version) next to you: near the end you scan a QR code with it, and the app asks you to confirm.

  1. Log in with the Facebook account that manages the TaxiBot business.
  2. Choose Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app and pick the business portfolio TaxiBot already uses.
  3. Enter the business number, then scan the QR code / accept the prompt in the app and choose to share chat history.
